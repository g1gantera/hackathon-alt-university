RAILFLOW · 5 / 12

A transparent dispatch preference

P = 4I + 2D + 3W − E

I = (importance−1)/9. D = min(positive delay/300 s,1). W = eligible wait/300 s. E = min(remaining traction kWh/1000,1).

Importance ranges from 1 to 10. Waiting has no cap.

Protected FIFO after 300 seconds. Departure time and train ID break ties. Mandatory constraints always apply.

RAILFLOW · 9 / 12

Driving advice and traction energy

Braking envelope: v ≤ √(vₜ² + 2bd), using SI units.

The dashboard compares recommended and actual speed and shows an arrival forecast.

ΔE (kWh) = [FΔx + max(0, ΔKE)] / (0.85 × 3,600,000).

The model includes rolling and aerodynamic resistance. It omits gradients and regenerative braking.

RAILFLOW · 10 / 12

Movement quality, explained

Q = 30S + 20C + 20E + 20F + 10A, each factor in [0,1].

S = max(0,1−mean positive delay/300 s). C = moving/eligible train samples.

E = min(1,reference/actual energy). F = 1−fraction of conflict samples.

A = max(0,1−maximum stop error/5 m). Arrival time belongs only to S.

Rolling 300-second window. No applicable observation gives a neutral factor of 1. No observations means no score.
